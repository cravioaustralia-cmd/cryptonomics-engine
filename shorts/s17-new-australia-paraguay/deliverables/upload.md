## Description
In 1893, with Australia in recession and striking shearers feeling beaten, journalist William Lane promised a socialist paradise in Paraguay. The ship Royal Tar left Sydney in July 1893 with over 200 colonists bound for "New Australia".

Lane's rules banned alcohol and mixing with Paraguayans, and only British settlers were allowed. The colony split within a year, and Lane's group left for Cosme. About 2,000 descendants still live in Paraguay.

One colonist was the poet Mary Gilmore, who's now on Australia's $10 note.
