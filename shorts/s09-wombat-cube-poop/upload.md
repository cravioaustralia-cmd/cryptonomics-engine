## Suggested title
This Animal Poops Perfect Cubes

## Description
The common wombat produces cube-shaped poo — up to a hundred cubes a night. But how do you get a cube out of a round hole? Scientists found the intestine walls are stiff in some spots and stretchy in others, squeezing the poo into corners. One theory for why: wombats leave them on rocks to mark territory, and cubes don't roll away. The research even won a prize.
