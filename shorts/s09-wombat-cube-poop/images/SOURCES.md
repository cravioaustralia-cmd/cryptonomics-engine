## Usage notes

- Do **not** invent AI stills of square anuses, fake wombat intestines, or Soft Matter paper figure recreations without a free licence.
- Digestive diagram: dim underlay; MG stiffness bands (`STIFF` / `STRETCHY`) carry the science claim. Never caption it as “wombat intestine photo.”
- Prize still: VO says scientists won a prize — real-world referent is the **2019 Ig Nobel Physics Prize** (Patricia Yang et al.). Keep MG as `PRIZE` / abstract award; do not paste Ig Nobel wordmarks unless a free-licence asset is verified later.
- Cube scat stills: respectful science/nature tone; avoid comedy “gross-out” zooms that fight the channel’s history-curiosity voice.
- CC BY-SA stills (Maria Island wombat, Dmitry Brant wombat, PooMuseum cubes, Certified faeces, Narawntapu burrow): keep attribution in this file; share-alike applies if a still itself is adapted as a standalone work — MG overlay on a dimmed underlay for the Short is the intended use.
