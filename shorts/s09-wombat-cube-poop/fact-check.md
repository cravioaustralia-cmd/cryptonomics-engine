# Fact-check notes — s09-wombat-cube-poop

Light check against published science / reporting as of 26 Sep 2026 (Australia/Sydney). Script claims were **not** expanded beyond the supplied VO. Australian English spelling preferred in captions (`faeces` / `poo` as VO uses; `metre` not required here).

## Claims that match published science

| VO claim | Support |
|---|---|
| This animal poops perfect cubes | Bare-nosed / common wombat (*Vombatus ursinus*) produces distinctive cube- or brick-shaped faeces — unique among mammals for clean-cut cubic scats. |
| It’s the wombat. Up to a hundred cubes. Every single night. | Widely reported ~80–100 cubes per night (e.g. Carver review; Conversation / Guardian / Smithsonian paraphrases). VO’s “up to a hundred” is fair. |
| How do you get a cube out of a round hole? | Cubes form **inside** the distal intestine / last ~17% of gut, then exit via a round anus — not a square anus. RSC / Soft Matter coverage emphasises this. |
| Scientists studied wombat intestines; walls stiff in some spots and stretchy in others, squeezing poo into corners | Yang et al., *Soft Matter* (2021): non-uniform intestinal stiffness (regions with ~2× thickness / ~4× stiffness differences) mould corners during contractions. Matches VO. |
| One theory: leave them on rocks to mark territory; cubes don’t roll away | Standard behavioural hypothesis (territorial / olfactory marking on rocks, logs, raised ground); cubes aggregate / stay put better than spheres. VO correctly hedges as “one theory.” |
| Scientists even won a prize for it | **2019 Ig Nobel Prize in Physics** awarded to Patricia Yang, Alexander Lee, Miles Chan, Alynn Martin, Ashley Edwards, Scott Carver, and David Hu for studying how and why wombats make cube-shaped poo. Soft Matter paper is the later journal write-up of that research programme. |

## Flags / nuance (do not invent into VO)

1. **“Perfect” cubes:** Real scats are cuboid / brick-like with slightly rounded edges — not machine-perfect cubes. VO comic hyperbole is fine; MG should not claim mathematical cubes.
2. **“Up to a hundred… every single night”:** Literature usually says ~80–100 per night. “Up to a hundred” is OK; avoid on-screen `EXACTLY 100` stamps.
3. **Territory / don’t-roll theory:** Widely cited and plausible; still framed as theory/hypothesis in careful writing. VO already says “one theory” — keep that hedge in captions.
4. **Prize = Ig Nobel Physics 2019 (Yang et al.):** Real. Still inventory uses a **generic PD science medallion**, not Ig Nobel branding — Claude should label MG `PRIZE` without inventing ceremony logos. Naming “Ig Nobel” on-screen is factually OK if desired; not required by VO (“a prize”).
5. **Digestive diagram still:** Human PD schematic stand-in only — not wombat histology from the paper (figures are copyrighted). Caption restraint: no “wombat gut photo.”
6. **Species:** Research focuses on bare-nosed / common wombat (*Vombatus ursinus*). Hairy-nosed species are related but not the VO’s referent — stick to common wombat stills (as supplied).

No contradictions found that require rewriting the supplied VO; flags are for caption / still restraint only.
