# Image sources — s21-broome-japanese-cemetery

**Mode:** Standard Skylab photo-underlay Short — **not** map-explainer. Correct geography: **Broome, Western Australia** (Japanese cemetery, Cable Beach, Roebuck Bay, period pearling). Soft-flag any non-Broome insert.

**Scaffold gaps for Claude (optional):** free-licence Broome copper-helmet-*on* period still; Wakayama/Taiji coastal still as brief origin chip only; Townsville Kardinia (first Japanese consulate) still if naming the city on the 1896 beat.
