- Halaib Triangle centre ≈ 22.6 N, 35.6 E (~20,580 km², Red Sea coast); Halaib town 22.22 N, 36.64 E; Jebel Elba 22.18 N, 36.37 E.
- 1899 "political" boundary = 22° N parallel (straight). 1902 "administrative" boundary per maps/images (map_egypt_sudan_claims_with_bir_tawil.png).
