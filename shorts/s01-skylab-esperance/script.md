## 1. Skylab vs Esperance
**On-screen hook:** "A town fined NASA for littering"
(deadpan) In 1979, a tiny Australian town fined NASA for littering.
(building) Here's why. NASA's first space station, Skylab, was falling out of orbit. Nobody knew exactly where it would land.
(dramatic) On 11 July it broke apart over Western Australia — and pieces of space station rained down near Esperance.
(amused) Locals collected the debris. One big chunk went into the town museum. And the Shire of Esperance did what any responsible council would do…
(punchline) it wrote NASA a $400 littering fine.
(pause then disbelief) NASA never paid.
(storytelling) For 30 years the fine just sat there. Then in 2009, American radio host Scott Barley raised the money from his listeners and paid it off.
(smiling) So if you ever drop something from space…
(loop) remember the tiny town that fined NASA.
**Fact notes:** Skylab re-entry 11 July 1979 over WA; Shire of Esperance issued $400 littering fine; unpaid until 2009 when Scott Barley (US radio) crowdfunded payment from listeners; debris displayed in Esperance museum.
