## Suggested title
A Tiny Australian Town Fined NASA for Littering

## Description
In 1979, Skylab fell over Western Australia. Chunks of NASA’s first space station landed near Esperance — so the Shire did what any responsible council would do: it wrote NASA a $400 littering fine.

NASA never paid. Thirty years later, US radio host Scott Barley and his listeners settled the tab.
