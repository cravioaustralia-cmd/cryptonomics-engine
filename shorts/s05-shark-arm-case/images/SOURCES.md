## Usage notes

- Never present the modern tiger-shark photos as the specific 1935 Coogee Aquarium specimen.
- Never present the boxers still as a photograph of James Smith.
- Never depict a severed arm, wound, or body; keep the knife and witness-car beats forensic and non-graphic.
- Coogee Pier is c.1929 context for the beach/aquarium district, not a 25 April 1935 eyewitness frame.
