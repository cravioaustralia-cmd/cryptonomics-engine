## Usage notes

- Do **not** invent AI stills of “hacked Medicare records”, patient charts, or Services Australia UI fakes.
- Avoid OpenAI wordmarks/logos as hero graphics (trademark risk for commercial YouTube); keep the agent beat on the neural-net schematic + MG.
- Coat of Arms: Commonwealth emblem is for factual AU-government context; do not imply Services Australia endorsement. Prefer muted/dim underlay with MG stamps (`HEALTH STATS`, `NO PATIENT RECORDS`).
- Albanese portrait: attribute DFAT per CC BY 4.0 on-screen credit or end-card if the channel’s credit style requires it.
- Parliament House (CC BY-SA 4.0): keep attribution in `SOURCES.md`; share-alike applies if the still itself is adapted as a standalone work — MG overlay on a dimmed underlay for the Short is the intended use.
- Electronic lock and cybersecurity padlock are abstract metaphors for “blocked” / “access”, not evidence of a specific exploit technique (see `fact-check.md`).
