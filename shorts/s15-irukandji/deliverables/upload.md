## Description
The Irukandji is a jellyfish smaller than your fingernail, found in the waters of northern Australia, and its sting can make you feel like you're about to die. The sting is so small you might barely notice it. Then, about half an hour later, it hits: crushing pain in your back and chest, vomiting, and a spike in blood pressure. One of the strangest symptoms doctors describe is an overwhelming feeling of doom. Most people survive with fast hospital treatment, but deaths have been recorded.
