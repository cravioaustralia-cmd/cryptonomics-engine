## Place-name confirmation (labels only — not added to the spoken script)

- **Cape Denison, Commonwealth Bay** is the base. Australian Antarctic Program: Mawson established the Australasian Antarctic Expedition headquarters at Commonwealth Bay in 1912; the huts stand at Cape Denison, Commonwealth Bay. https://www.antarctica.gov.au/about-antarctica/history/cultural-heritage/mawsons-huts-cape-denison/ (fetched 4 Oct 2026). Wikipedia *Cape Denison*: rocky point at the head of Commonwealth Bay, George V Land; site of the expedition’s main base; about 67°00′S, 142°40′E. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Denison
- **Mertz Glacier** — George V Coast; named for Xavier Mertz; about 67°30′S, 144°45′E. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mertz_Glacier (fetched 4 Oct 2026).
- **Ninnis Glacier** — George V Coast; named for Lieutenant B. E. S. Ninnis, who died on the far-eastern sledge journey when he fell into a crevasse on this glacier; about 68°22′S, 147°00′E. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninnis_Glacier (fetched 4 Oct 2026).
- Do **not** label the ship, “Black Crevasse”, “14 December 1912”, “8 February 1913”, “Aurora”, or “Far Eastern Party”. The script does not say them.
