## NSW involvement (note for Claude / docs — VO may stay SA–Vic)

**SOFT — include in docs; optional in VO.**  
In 1836 the land east of SA was still **New South Wales**; Victoria did not yet exist as a separate colony (Port Phillip / Vic separation 1851). So the original eastern border was **SA–NSW**. After remeasurement, **NSW and SA accepted** a realignment onto the true 141st meridian for their shared border; **Victoria refused** a matching shift. The Privy Council case is styled *South Australia v Victoria*, but the story is incomplete without the NSW chapter.  
**Suggested soft VO option (if a beat is added):** “It started as a border with New South Wales — Victoria inherited the fight.”
