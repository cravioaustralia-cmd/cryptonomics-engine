## Usage notes

- Prefer Queensland / Wet Tropics southern cassowary stills for Australian VO claims.
- Claw still: science/nature tone; dagger MG outline OK; **never** wound recreation.
- 1926 beat: Cook Highway still is era/region context only — label `1926` / `QUEENSLAND`; do not caption as “McClean’s farm.”
- Seed dropping: respectful ecology photography (seeds visible) — not gross-out comedy.
- CC BY / BY-SA stills: keep attribution in this file; share-alike applies if a still itself is adapted as a standalone work — MG overlay on a dimmed underlay for the Short is the intended use.
