## Description
Australia has a bird that looks like a dinosaur. The southern cassowary stands up to about 2 m tall, runs at around 50 km/h, and has a dagger-like claw over 10 cm long on each foot. In 1926 one killed a teenager in Queensland, one of the very few human deaths ever recorded from a bird. But cassowaries are shy, and they are rainforest gardeners: they spread the seeds of plants that almost nothing else can swallow.
