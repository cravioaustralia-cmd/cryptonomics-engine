Facts (for pinned comment): joint sovereignty; administration alternates — Spain 1 February to 31 July, France 1 August to 31 January (Bayonne convention, 1901). Treaty of the Pyrenees signed on the island 7 November 1659. Area about 6,820 m². Sources: Wikipedia "Pheasant Island"; BBC, 28 Jan 2018; French Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs notice.
