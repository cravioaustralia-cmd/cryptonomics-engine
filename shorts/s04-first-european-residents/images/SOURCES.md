## Usage notes

- The Batavia replica is not the 1629 original; keep the replica label in the final treatment.
- The fort is a present-day photograph of the surviving remains, not a reconstruction of the 1629 walls.
- The plate is public-domain source material and should not be presented as a photograph of Cornelisz or the killings.
