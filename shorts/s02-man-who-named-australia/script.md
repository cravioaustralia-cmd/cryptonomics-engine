## Spoken script

(mysterious) The man who gave Australia its name… was lost under a train station for 200 years.

(storytelling) Matthew Flinders was the first person known to sail all the way around Australia. His best friend on board? (warm) A black cat named Trim.

(serious) On the way home, the French imprisoned Flinders on Mauritius for six and a half years. Trim disappeared there.

(building) Back in England, Flinders pushed for one name for the continent: Australia. His book came out in July 1814. (sad) He died the very next day.

(mysterious) He was buried in London. Then the city grew, the headstone was removed, and a train station swallowed the cemetery. His grave was lost.

(excited) Until 2019. Archaeologists digging for a new railway found 40,000 burials… and one lead plate reading “Captain Matthew Flinders.”

(soft) In 2024, he was finally laid to rest in his home village. (loop) Not bad for the man who named Australia.
