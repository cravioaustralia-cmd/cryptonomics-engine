(storytelling) His name is Bert Hinkler. He grew up in a small Australian town, watching birds and building his own gliders.

(deadpan) Basically, he watches birds… then tries to become one.

(tense) Nobody has ever flown this far alone. The fastest anyone has done it, even with a full crew, is 28 days.

(tense) Burma. Singapore. Then down through the islands of today’s Indonesia.

(slow, eerie) For over three months, nobody knows where he is. Then his body is found beside his crashed plane, on a mountain in Italy.

(twist) And Italy’s leader at the time, the dictator Benito Mussolini, orders a funeral with full military honours.
