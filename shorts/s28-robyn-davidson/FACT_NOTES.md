# Fact notes — s28 Robyn Davidson (do not add to the spoken script)

Checked 3 Oct 2026. The spoken script is locked.

## Endpoint (map label only)

The VO says only “the ocean” and “Australia’s west coast.”  
National Portrait Gallery of Australia, portrait record **2014.9**, titles Rick Smolan’s 1977 photograph “Robyn Davidson (Hamelin Pool, Indian Ocean, Western Australia)” and says she trained the camels for a trek “to Shark Bay” and arrived “over 2,700 kilometres” and “nine months” later.  
https://www.portrait.gov.au/portraits/2014.9/robyn-davidson-hamelin-pool-indian-ocean-western-australia

Use that if a map pin needs a real shore. **Do not say Hamelin Pool or Shark Bay in the VO.**  
**Do not use Hamelin Bay** (a different place, south-west WA). **Do not use Steep Point** — that was not what the NPG record says.

## Nine months vs day 195

- Standard published length: nine months (Wikipedia; NPG; BBC Travel, 6 Apr 2015).
- *The Telegraph* film package reprints her account: friends trucked her to Glen Helen, 80 miles west of Alice, and she left the Redbank area on **8 April**; near the end it numbers **“DAY 195.”** The same package also says “1,700 miles in nine months.”
- 8 April plus 195 days lands in mid-October, which is not nine calendar months. The scaffold does **not** know whether “day 195” counts from Alice, from Glen Helen, or from a later zero, or whether “nine months” includes the last weeks of preparation. **Leave the spoken line as written.**

## Names kept out of the VO on purpose

- Photographer: **Rick Smolan** (free 2009 portrait only; no NatGeo scan).
- Dog: **Diggity** (NPG) / **Diggitty** (Wikipedia). Spelling disagrees. Do not add either to the VO. Do not depict the shooting.
- Elder: **Mr Eddie**, Pitjantjatjara, Docker River toward Warburton (Australian Museum; Wikipedia, citing the book). Do not add the name to the VO. No sacred art, no caricature.
- Camels in the sources: Dookie, Bub, Zeleika, Goliath. Not required on screen. Do not speak them.
- Handler often credited: **Sallay Mahomet**, Alice Springs. Not in the VO. Do not add him unless a later brief says so.
- Departure detail not in the VO: the walk proper is often counted from Glen Helen / Redbank Gorge, west of Alice, rather than from the town centre. The VO says she arrives in Alice Springs with the plan to walk west. That is fair. A map may start the drawn line at Alice Springs as the VO does.

## Portrait gap

No free-licence photograph of Robyn Davidson was found. She was born in 1950 and is living, so 1977 photographs are not public domain in Australia. Do not generate one.
