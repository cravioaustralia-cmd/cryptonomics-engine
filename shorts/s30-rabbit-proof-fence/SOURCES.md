## Place-name confirmation (labels only — not added to the spoken script)

- **Jigalong** is the remote desert community. Molly Craig (Molly Kelly) was born there and the 1931 walk with Daisy Kadibil and Gracie Cross was home to Jigalong, after removal to the Moore River Native Settlement. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molly_Craig (fetched 4 Oct 2026).
- **Moore River** is that government camp near Perth. Same Wikipedia page, and the AIATSIS catalogue record for Doris Pilkington, *Follow the Rabbit-Proof Fence* (call B P639.20/F1), which places the removal at Jigalong and the settlement at Moore River: https://aiatsis.gov.au/collections/item/M0015130
- The script’s “nearby town” is **not labelled**. Daughter names are **not labelled**.
