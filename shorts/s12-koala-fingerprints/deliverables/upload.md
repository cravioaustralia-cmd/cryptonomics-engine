## Description
Koalas have fingerprints almost exactly like yours: the same loops, whorls and ridges. Under a microscope, researchers found them incredibly hard to tell apart from human prints. In theory, a koala print at a crime scene could look like a person's, though scientists have said that is extremely unlikely. We aren't even closely related. Koalas evolved fingerprints completely separately, probably for gripping branches and eucalyptus leaves. It's a classic case of convergent evolution.
