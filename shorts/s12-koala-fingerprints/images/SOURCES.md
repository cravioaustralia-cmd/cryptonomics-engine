## Usage notes

- Prefer Australian habitat stills for VO claims.
- Hind-foot pad still: science/nature tone + SVG ridge overlay OK; never caption as a recovered crime-scene print.
- Crime-scene beat: NIST card is a **print motif** only — label `IN THEORY`; do not invent a real case.
- Microscope still: generic lab proxy — do not caption as Henneberg’s Adelaide SEM session.
- Free-licence front-pad micrographs of koala fingertip dermatoglyphs were not available; Claude may SVG-draw ridge/loop overlays on the pad still + human print compares.
- CC BY / BY-SA stills: keep attribution in this file; share-alike applies if a still itself is adapted as a standalone work — MG overlay on a dimmed underlay for the Short is the intended use.
