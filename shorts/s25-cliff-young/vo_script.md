(fast, amazed) In 1983, a 61-year-old potato farmer showed up to one of the toughest races on Earth… wearing overalls and gumboots.

(amused) His name is Cliff Young. He only started running in his late fifties.

(storytelling) The race: Sydney to Melbourne, on foot. 875 kilometres. Against professional runners half his age.

(deadpan) The pros have sponsors, coaches and fancy shoes. Cliff has… gumboots.

(sly) People laugh. Some reportedly think he’s just there to watch.

(amused) The race starts. The pros speed off. Cliff does a strange, slow shuffle, like a man looking for his car keys.

(tense) That night, the pros stop to sleep. Cliff keeps shuffling.

(amused) Reportedly, someone in his team sets his alarm wrong, so he sleeps only about two hours. Best mistake ever.

(fast) By morning, the news spreads: the old farmer is still going.

(sly) His secret training? He reportedly said he used to round up sheep on foot… for days.

(deadpan) He does swap the gumboots for running shoes. Ten pairs, by the end.

(awed) Day after day, he sleeps a few hours at a time… and slowly catches the leaders.

(twist) After 5 days, 15 hours and 4 minutes, Cliff crosses the finish line in Melbourne. First.

(dramatic) Then his legs give out, and he has to be carried off the stage.

(twist, warm) He wins $10,000. And by most accounts, he shares it with the other runners and his team.
