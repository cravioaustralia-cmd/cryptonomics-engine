# Fact-check — s10 Shark Arm Case

Status: **PASS** for comic/true-crime Short framing (same core facts as s05).

## Claims vs record
- **1935 / Sydney aquarium / tiger shark regurgitated a human arm in front of spectators:** Yes — Coogee Aquarium Baths, April–May 1935; tiger shark caught off Coogee; vomited a human forearm before a crowd.
- **Tattoo of two boxers; arm cut with a knife (not bitten):** Yes — police/forensic finding; tattoo identified with missing man James Smith (ex-boxer).
- **Treated as murder; shark as “delivery”:** Fair vernacular; the case is the famous “Shark Arm murder.”
- **Suspect arrested; key witness shot dead in car; suspect walked free:** Yes — Patrick Brady charged; Reginald Holmes (key figure/witness) found shot in his car; Brady acquitted.
- **Body never found; punchline that the shark was the only “witness” to the evidence:** Comic framing of a real unsolved/body-never-found case — OK for Shorts.

## Guardrails for Claude
- No gore / severed-arm photography / wound / body recreation.
- Present-day tiger-shark stills are wildlife stand-ins, not the 1935 specimen.
- Boxers still is tattoo/metaphor only — not a portrait of James Smith.
- Knife / “cut off” beats stay forensic language + labels, never graphic.
