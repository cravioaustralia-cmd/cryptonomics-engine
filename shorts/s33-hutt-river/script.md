`(fast, intrigued)` In 1970, a West Australian farmer quit Australia… without leaving his farm.

`(storytelling)` Leonard Casley had about four thousand hectares of wheat ready to harvest.

`(indignant)` Then new government quotas let him sell the crop from roughly forty.

`(defiant)` So on the twenty-first of April, he declared his farm an independent country.

`(amused)` The Hutt River Province. Seventy-five square kilometres, about five hundred kilometres north of Perth.

`(building)` He started calling himself Prince Leonard. Printed his own stamps. Minted his own coins. Issued his own passports.

`(deadpan)` In 1977, he even declared war on Australia… then called it off a few days later.

`(serious)` But no country ever recognised it. And the tax office never stopped counting.

## Fact notes (description / pinned comment, NOT spoken)

- Leonard Casley declared his farm near Northampton, WA, the "Hutt River Province" on 21 April 1970 over a dispute about wheat production quotas. He had ~4,000 ha of wheat ready to harvest; the quota let him sell 1,647 bushels, roughly 40 ha worth. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principality_of_Hutt_River (citing ABC, 21 Apr 2015)
- ~75 km², ~517 km north of Perth. Issued its own currency, stamps and passports; never recognised by Australia or any other government. — same
- 2 December 1977: Casley "declared war" on Australia, notifying authorities of the cessation of hostilities several days later. — same
- Began styling himself "Prince Leonard" a few years after 1970. Abdicated to son Graeme 11 Feb 2017 — https://www.abc.net.au/news/2017-02-02/prince-leonard-of-hutt-river-principality-abdicates-to-son/8232730 ; died 13 Feb 2019 aged 93 — https://www.abc.net.au/news/2019-02-13/prince-leonard-who-founded-hutt-river-province-dies-aged-93/10808236
- June 2017: WA Supreme Court ordered Leonard to pay ~A$2.7m and son Arthur ~A$242,000 in unpaid tax (~A$3m total). — https://www.abc.net.au/news/2017-06-16/hutt-river-province-decision-on-ato-fight/8624450
- Dissolved / rejoined Australia 3 August 2020 amid tax debts and COVID impacts; land sold to settle the tax dispute. — https://www.watoday.com.au/national/end-of-an-empire-hutt-river-to-rejoin-australia-after-50-years-20200803-p55i1u.html
