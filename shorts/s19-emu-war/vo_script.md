(deadpan, fast) Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight birds… and lost.

(storytelling) 1932. About 20,000 emus are wrecking wheat farms in Western Australia. So the army sends soldiers with machine guns.

(comedic, building) Problem one: the emus split up and scatter. Problem two: they run over 40 kilometres an hour. Problem three: many kept running even after being hit.

(laughing) After weeks, thousands of bullets, and newspapers mocking them… the soldiers were pulled out.

(punchline) The officer in charge reportedly said that with an army of birds like these, he could take on any army in the world.

(loop) Which is how Australia sent soldiers to fight birds… and lost.
