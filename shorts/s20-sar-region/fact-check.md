## Claim checklist

### 1. “Australia coordinates rescues across one-tenth of the entire planet.”
**PASS (soft).**  
AMSA (and NATSAR / ANAO): Australian SRR is **nearly 53 million km²** and **one tenth of the Earth’s surface**. Soft: “one-tenth” matches AMSA wording; keep soft — do not invent a harder fraction on screen. Soft: “coordinates rescues” = AMSA JRCC coordination responsibility under ICAO/IMO — OK; do not imply Australia alone physically performs every rescue asset movement in the zone.

### 2. “Including some places you’d never guess.”
**PASS (editorial colour).**  
Sets up Antarctic foreign bases twist. Not a hard geographic claim.

### 3. “It’s called a search and rescue region. Countries split the world’s oceans into zones like this by international agreement.”
**PASS.**  
SRR = Search and Rescue Region under **ICAO** (Annex 12) and **IMO** (1979 SAR Convention / IAMSAR). Oceans partitioned into SRRs by international agreement so a responsible RCC exists worldwide. Soft: land SAR inside Australia is shared with state/territory police — VO focuses on the named **search and rescue region** / ocean-zone framing — OK.

### 4. “Australia’s covers about 53 million square kilometres.”
**PASS.**  
AMSA / NATSAR / ANAO: **nearly 53 million** km²; NATSAR prelims also cite ≈**52.8 million** km². Soft **“about 53 million”** is exactly right — keep “about”; **FAIL** only if flashing an invented exact figure without soft wording. On-screen prefer `~53 MILLION KM²` / `ABOUT 53M KM²`.

### 5. “Roughly halfway to Africa. Halfway to Indonesia. Halfway to New Zealand.”
**PASS.**  
AMSA Media Centre backgrounder uses this **exact** scale framing: *“it’s about halfway to Africa, halfway to Indonesia, halfway to New Zealand and all the way to the South Pole.”* Soft: keep **“Roughly” / about**; these are AMSA’s illustrative extent claims, not great-circle capital-to-capital distances — do **not** invent hard km arcs to Cape Town / Jakarta / Wellington as gospel unless labelled soft. Map treatment: extent arrows / halfway badges from Australian landmass toward those directions matching AMSA colour.

### 6. “And all the way down to the South Pole.”
**PASS-SOFT (aviation SRR).**  
NATSAR §1.5 note: **maritime SRR extends to the Antarctic coast**; **aviation SRR extends to the South Pole**. AMSA media uses “all the way to the South Pole.” VO matches AMSA public wording / aviation SRR. Soft on-screen: OK to show aviation SRR to Pole; if distinguishing maritime vs aviation, prefer soft note in MG only if needed — do **not** contradict VO. Soft: do not imply maritime surface SAR assets routinely operate at the Pole.

### 7. “It borders the rescue zones of countries from South Africa to Sri Lanka to the Solomon Islands.”
**PASS-SOFT.**  
AMSA: SRR **borders the search and rescue regions of 10 other countries**. VO is a **west → NW → east sweep**, not an exhaustive list.  
- **Sri Lanka:** AMSA states Maldives / Mauritius / Sri Lanka SAR regions are **adjacent** to the remote NW corner of Australia’s SRR (SCPP).  
- **Solomon Islands:** Pacific neighbour; Australia–Solomon Islands SAR arrangement documented; eastern SRR reaches ~163°E.  
- **South Africa:** Australia’s western Antarctic/Indian Ocean SRR edge at **75°E** meets the African / Indian Ocean SAR partition historically associated with South African / adjacent African-region responsibility — fair soft sweep endpoint; do **not** hard-claim South Africa is the sole western neighbour or invent a shared land border. Soft: on-screen may label `SOUTH AFRICA` · `SRI LANKA` · `SOLOMON ISLANDS` as example border chips + optional `10 NEIGHBOURS` soft badge — do not invent a false complete list of all 10 without a sourced list.

### 8. “And inside it sit Antarctic bases run by other nations, including Russia’s Vostok station and the French-Italian Concordia.”
**PASS-SOFT (verify geography carefully — staging OK).**  
- **Vostok** (Russia): ≈78°28′S **106°48′E** — longitude inside Australian SRR band **75°E–163°E**; sits in the Australian Antarctic Territory claim (Wilkes Land / Princess Elizabeth Land sector).  
- **Concordia** (France–Italy): ≈75°06′S **123°20′E** — likewise inside 75°E–163°E; commonly described as within the **Australian Antarctic Territory**; French–Italian year-round plateau station.  
Aviation SRR covers inland ice to the Pole, so both stations sit **inside** the Australian **aviation** SRR / AAT geographic box. Soft: Antarctic Treaty — territorial claims suspended for operational purposes; bases are run by their national programs. Soft: National Antarctic Programs often provide first-response SAR locally; AMSA JRCC retains international coordination responsibility for the SRR — VO “inside it sit” is geographic/SRR-responsibility colour, **not** a claim that AMSA staffs those bases. Soft: keep “French-Italian Concordia” (joint FR/IT). **FAIL** only if pinning stations outside 75–163°E or claiming they are Australian-operated.

### 9. Incomplete loop — “So if something goes wrong there,”
**PASS (editorial).**  
Incomplete on purpose; cycles to the open. Not a factual claim by itself. Claude owns visual whip-back.
