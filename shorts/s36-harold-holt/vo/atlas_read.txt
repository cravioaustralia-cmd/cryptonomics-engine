In 1967, Australia's Prime Minister went for a swim… and was never seen again.

Harold Holt loved the ocean. On the seventeenth of December, he waded into rough surf at Cheviot Beach, near Portsea in Victoria.

The only other swimmer felt the undertow and stayed close to shore. Holt kept swimming out.

Then he slipped under the waves… and was gone.

Divers, helicopters, police, soldiers and sailors combed the coast. One of the biggest searches in Australian history.

His body was never found.

Rumours ran wild. One book even claimed a Chinese submarine picked him up.

In 2005, a coroner ruled he simply drowned.

His name even became slang. To "do a Harold Holt" means to bolt.

And in 1969, a Melbourne council opened a brand-new pool: the Harold Holt Memorial Swimming Centre.
