# Cravio AI: sales video scripts (motion graphics only)
*Drafted 16 Oct 2026 (AEDT). Sources: `/workspace/biz-ideas/ai-consultancy.md` and the live preview https://cravioai-200.netlify.app. Australian English. No stats, clients, testimonials or prices are claimed.*

## Global production rules
- **Motion graphics only.** Kinetic type, line icons, animated diagrams and UI mockups. No live footage, presenters, hands, faces or silhouettes of people. "Your team" shows up as a cyan **approval tick** or an "Approve" button, never as a person.
- **Brand:** navy `#0A1628` backgrounds, cyan `#00C2B2` accents, white type. Space Grotesk throughout (Bold for headlines, Medium for UI). Logo: the "circuit track" C.
- **Visual motif:** a **conveyor/circuit track**. Cyan lines run like PCB traces and carry work items (emails, quotes, posts) from station to station like a production line. This makes "AI that runs like a factory" literal.
- **UI mockups:** generic, unbranded inbox, quote, chat and calendar panels. Use fictional or placeholder data only (e.g. "Part #EX-2041", "Customer: Sample Pty Ltd"). Use no real client names and no third-party logos. If Xero, MYOB and so on are shown, use plain text labels only, matching the site's "compatibility only" note.
- **Pacing:** VO at about 2.5 words/sec. Every scene's word count is checked in the tables below.
- **Music:** modern industrial-electronic. Muted kick, ticking hi-hat in 16ths like a machine, warm synth pad, rising at the CTA. About 110–120 BPM so cuts land on the beat. Use licensed stock or library music.
- **VO:** an Australian voice, warm and confident, not salesy. Male or female.

## Fact checks and open questions
1. **Audit length:** the brief says "30-minute"; the site says "30–45 minute". The scripts say "30-minute" to match the brief. Change it to "free AI audit" (no duration) if you'd rather not conflict with the site.
2. **Booking:** the site says "Online booking isn't live yet: email or call us". A "book at cravioai.com" CTA works only once booking is live, or if the site's contact form or phone counts as booking. Also confirm that cravioai.com (not just the Netlify preview) is live before publishing.
3. **Phone in the VO:** the VO uses the domestic format "0478 953 337" (natural for Australian listeners) and the screen shows +61. Check this is the same mobile.
4. **MB Enterprises** is deliberately not named, and there's no claim that it was "proven in our own factory", because the site says Australian case studies aren't published yet. "Built by a manufacturing family" is used as given.
5. "Content that runs itself" (social, scene 3) is a slogan line, immediately qualified by human approval. Swap it for "a content engine on autopilot" if you prefer.
6. Example UI data must stay generic or fictional. Don't use real part numbers or customer names from MB's catalogue unless you have permission.
