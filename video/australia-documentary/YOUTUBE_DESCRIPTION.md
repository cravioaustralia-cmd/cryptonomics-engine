# YouTube description (draft)

From giant lizards and the world's oldest living cultures to convict ships, gold rushes, a war against emus and modern Australia: the entire history of a remarkable country, told in about 15 minutes.

We acknowledge the Traditional Custodians of Country throughout Australia and their continuing connection to land, sea and sky. We pay our respects to Elders past and present.

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander viewers are advised that this video contains images and names of people who have died.

A note on accuracy: this video is based on historical records and the best research available to us. History is vast and new evidence keeps coming to light. If we've got something wrong, it wasn't intentional. Let us know in the comments and we'll pin corrections.
