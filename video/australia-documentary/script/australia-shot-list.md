# Shot List: The Entire History of Australia

Times match `australia-history-final-mix.mp3` (the audio timeline). In the finished video, everything shifts later by the length of the opening cards. Save each image with its number first (for example `01_emu.jpg`, `02_prison-ship.jpg`) so they stay in order. Landscape images (wider than tall) work best. Aim for at least 1920 pixels wide.

**Source key:** PX = Pixabay / Pexels / Unsplash · WC = Wikimedia Commons · TR = Trove (National Library of Australia) · SL = State Library of NSW or Victoria · AWM = Australian War Memorial · NAA = National Archives of Australia

Always check the licence or rights statement on each image. On Wikimedia Commons, "Public domain" is safest; "CC BY" or "CC BY-SA" is fine but needs a credit in your video description.
