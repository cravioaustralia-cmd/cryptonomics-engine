## Evidence labels (script Part 2 — every card and key line carries one)

- **FACT** (white): verified events, official results, records.
- **CLAIM** (orange outline): what a person/party says (e.g. One Nation's "Fire the Liar" figures, Albanese's plane quote, government's stated focus).
- **ANALYSIS** (blue #3A7BD5): attributed interpretation by a named analyst/outlet (ABC "fastest polling surge"; ABC on Labor's ad strategy; Grattan on the tax decision; ABC on immigration message).
- **SPECULATION** (grey, dashed): what could happen — **ending only** (S39 "UNKNOWN" column, S40 Victorian test node).
- Every poll card (Polaroid): pollster, fieldwork dates, sample size where known, and "One poll is a snapshot, not a verdict."
- No winner declared; no invented motives (S33 "MOTIVE?" card → "interpretation, not fact").
