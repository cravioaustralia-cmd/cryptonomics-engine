The second big change isn’t about One Nation at all. It’s about the Coalition. After the twenty twenty-five defeat, the Liberal Party recorded its worst federal vote share since its founding. The Nationals walked out of the Coalition twice, in May twenty twenty-five and again in January twenty twenty-six, before reuniting. The Liberals replaced Sussan Ley with Angus Taylor as leader in February twenty twenty-six. And in Newspoll’s latest quarterly breakdown, One Nation was ahead of the Coalition in all five mainland states.
