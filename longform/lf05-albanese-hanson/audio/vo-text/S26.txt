Start with what Anthony Albanese and Labor have actually done. In June twenty twenty-six, Labor began asking supporters online for donations to help stop One Nation, including requests for twenty-seven dollars. By August, according to ABC analysis, Labor’s ads had a clear strategy: rather than simply attacking Pauline Hanson, they tried to tie One Nation and the Coalition together in voters’ minds, and warned that the Coalition couldn’t govern without One Nation. One series of ads targeted Hanson’s suggestion that people be allowed to access their superannuation when struggling with the cost of living. Another targeted her comments about paid parental leave.
