Then there’s One Nation’s relationship with the Coalition. Opposition Leader Angus Taylor has ruled out forming a coalition with One Nation, telling the ABC’s seven thirty that a One Nation government would bring “an eternity of pain”. But Taylor has not ruled out preference deals. The Coalition voted against censuring Hanson in March. And former Liberal prime minister Tony Abbott, who once funded legal action against One Nation, now says that, as a general rule, parties on the right should preference each other.
