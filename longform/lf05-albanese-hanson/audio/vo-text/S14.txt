If you’ve seen our previous documentary on Pauline Hanson, you’ll know her story is one of rises and falls. Pauline Hanson entered Federal Parliament in nineteen ninety-six. She founded One Nation in nineteen ninety-seven, and the next year the party won almost twenty-three per cent of the vote and eleven seats in the Queensland state election. Then it fell apart. Hanson lost her federal seat, was jailed for electoral fraud, and had the conviction overturned on appeal. She spent more than a decade losing elections, before returning to the Senate in twenty sixteen.
