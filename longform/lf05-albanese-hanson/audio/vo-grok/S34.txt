To understand whether Pauline Hanson’s poll numbers threaten Anthony Albanese’s government, you need to know how Australian elections actually work. Popularity, polling, seats and government are four different things. [pause] A party’s primary vote is just the first number on the ballot. The House of Representatives has one hundred and fifty seats, each won by the candidate who gets more than half the vote after preferences are counted. To form government, a party needs seventy-six of those seats.
