That means where votes are matters as much as how many there are. A party on thirty per cent nationally can win very few seats if its votes are spread evenly, or many seats if they’re concentrated. In nineteen ninety-eight, One Nation won almost twenty-three per cent of the Queensland vote and took eleven of eighty-nine state seats.
