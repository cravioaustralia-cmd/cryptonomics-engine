What happened next was much faster. In late twenty twenty-five, former deputy prime minister Barnaby Joyce left the Nationals and joined One Nation. In early twenty twenty-six, Newspoll recorded One Nation ahead of the Coalition for the first time. In March, One Nation won four lower-house seats in the South Australian state election. In May, One Nation’s David Farley won the federal seat of Farrer, the party’s first ever win in the federal lower house. [pause] And by September, Newspoll had One Nation in first place, on thirty per cent.
