The Senate works differently. Each state elects senators by proportional representation, so at a normal half-Senate election, a party needs about one-seventh of a state’s vote, roughly fourteen per cent, to win a seat. That’s why minor parties like One Nation have usually done far better in the Senate. And preferences decide close contests. If Coalition voters preference One Nation above Labor, One Nation’s chances rise. [pause] If they don’t, they fall. [pause] That’s why the preference question matters so much.
