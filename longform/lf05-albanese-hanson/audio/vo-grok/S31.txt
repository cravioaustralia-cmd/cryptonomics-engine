Pauline Hanson has also changed One Nation’s target. After the Farrer win, she posted that One Nation had proven it could win lower-house seats, and that it was time to target Labor-held seats. On policy, Hanson has said One Nation would cut net migration to one hundred and thirty thousand a year. She has floated letting people access their superannuation during cost-of-living pressure. And One Nation has relaunched its animated series, “Please Explain”, released on its YouTube channel and rebroadcast on Sky News.
