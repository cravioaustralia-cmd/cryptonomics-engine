Then there’s One Nation’s relationship with the Coalition. Opposition Leader Angus Taylor has ruled out forming a coalition with One Nation, telling the ABC’s seven thirty that a One Nation government would bring “an eternity of pain”. [pause] But Taylor has not ruled out preference deals. [pause] The Coalition voted against censuring Hanson in March. And former Liberal prime minister Tony Abbott, who once funded legal action against One Nation, now says that, as a general rule, parties on the right should preference each other.
