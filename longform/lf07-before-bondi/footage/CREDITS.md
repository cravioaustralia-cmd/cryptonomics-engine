## Licence flags
- DVIDS (FT01): not public-domain-by-name on the page; says US-Government employee works are not copyrighted in the US but "must comply with the restrictions" page. "Commercial use" is defined as advertising/marketing/promotion/fundraising; a documentary is not obviously covered, but a monetised YouTube channel might be argued either way — the licence text does not name YouTube. Mandatory non-endorsement disclaimer. Unit insignia / tail numbers are "requested" to be obscured only for commercial use; the clip shows no legible markings at this crop.
- Mixkit licence explicitly lists YouTube videos. CC BY 3.0 (ST04/ST05) does not name YouTube; attribution required.
- UPGRADE PASS licence flags: every new clip is a Wikimedia Commons file under CC BY 3.0 / CC BY 4.0 / CC BY-SA 3.0 / CC BY-SA 4.0 / public domain. None of these licences names YouTube; they all allow commercial use and monetised video with attribution. CC BY-SA items (ST07, ST09, ST12, ST21, plus PH06c etc. in images) carry a share-alike condition on the adapted material itself (the individual clip/photo), not on the whole film — credit them and say the clip is adapted (cropped/trimmed).
- Coverr licence (checked, nothing seated): "Use Content for Free … for both commercial and non-commercial purposes … no attribution required" but bans use for AI training/datasets; no Sydney or relevant clips found, so no Coverr files used.
