DOC02 — Roll-call vote, 29 November 1947 (UN General Assembly, 128th plenary meeting)
Symbol: A/PV.128
Official PDF: https://documents.un.org/doc/undoc/gen/nl4/717/10/pdf/nl471710.pdf
Page URL (symbol resolver): https://docs.un.org/en/A/PV.128   (API: https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?s=A/PV.128&l=en&t=pdf)
Meeting: "Held in the General Assembly Hall at Flushing Meadow, New York, on Saturday, 29 November 1947, at 4 p.m.
          President: Mr. O. ARANHA (Brazil)."

Roll-call result — printed page 1424-1425 (PDF pp.15-16), English column, exact:
  "We shall now proceed to vote by roll-call on the report of the Ad Hoc Committee (document A/516).
   A vote was taken by roll-call. The result of the vote was as follows:
   In favour: Australia, Belgium, Bolivia, Brazil, Byelorussian Soviet Socialist Republic, Canada,
   Costa Rica, Czechoslovakia, Denmark, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, France, Guatemala, Haiti, Iceland,
   Liberia, Luxembourg, Netherlands, New Zealand, Nicaragua, Norway, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Philippines,
   Poland, Sweden, Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic, Union of South Africa, Union of Soviet Socialist
   Republics, United States of America, Uruguay, Venezuela.
   Against: Afghanistan, Cuba, Egypt, Greece, India, Iran, Iraq, Lebanon, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, Syria,
   Turkey, Yemen.
   Abstained: Argentina, Chile, China, Colombia, El Salvador, Ethiopia, Honduras, Mexico, United Kingdom,
   Yugoslavia.
   The report was adopted by 33 votes to 13, with 10 abstentions."

Calling order (Afghanistan -> Argentina -> Australia):
  A/PV.128 prints the RESULT grouped by Yes/No/Abstain, not the calling sequence. The calling sequence
  (English alphabetical, starting at Afghanistan) is evidenced by:
   1. The UN voting sheet "UNITED NATIONS GENERAL ASSEMBLY  November 29, 1947  VOTING SHEET"
      (DOC02b_voting_sheet_29nov1947.jpg; Commons File:Votes-list_Resolution_181-1947.jpg, PD-US-no notice-UN):
      AFGHANISTAN  No (X) / ARGENTINA  Abstain (*) / AUSTRALIA  Yes (tick); footer "Yes 33  No 13  Abstain 10  Absent 1";
      SIAM marked in the Absent column.
   2. Transcript of the original UN audio (IsraCast, https://www.isracast.com/november-29th-israel-palestinians-where-it-all-began/):
      "Afghanistan? No. Argentina? Argentina? Abstention. Australia? Yes. Belgium? Yes. ..."
   3. New York Times, 30 Nov 1947 (https://archive.nytimes.com/www.nytimes.com/learning/general/onthisday/big/1129.html):
      "For (33) - Australia, ... Absent (1) - Siam."
  => "the first country to vote yes is Australia" = VERIFIED.

Same record, printed p.1413 (PDF p.4), Rapporteur Thors (Iceland): "the Chairman of the Ad Hoc Committee, Mr. Evatt; ...
  Unfortunately, Mr. Evatt has now left for Australia." (Evatt chaired the committee but was NOT in the hall for the vote.)
