  Schedule 3 inserts Criminal Code sections 474.17C and 474.17D. Act made 10 Dec 2024; Schedule 3 commenced 11 Dec 2024 (per search index; confirm in the Act's commencement table, s 2).
Exact wording (s 474.17C(1)): "A person commits an offence if: (a) the person uses a carriage service to make available, publish or otherwise distribute information; and (b) the information is personal data of one or more individuals; and (c) the person engages in the conduct in a way that reasonable persons would regard as being, in all the circumstances, menacing or harassing towards those individuals."  Penalty: Imprisonment for 6 years.
  Note to s 474.17C: "Publishing the name, image and telephone number of an individual on a website and encouraging others to repeatedly contact the individual with violent or threatening messages is an example of conduct (commonly referred to as doxxing)".
  s 474.17D = group-based doxxing (members of a group because of a protected attribute); penalty 7 years (search index; check text).
