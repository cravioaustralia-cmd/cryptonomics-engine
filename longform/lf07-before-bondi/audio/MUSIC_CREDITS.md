# BEFORE BONDI (lf07): music and sound-effect credits

Built 10 Oct 2026 (AEDT). 33 files: 9 music in `audio/music/`, 24 effects in `audio/sfx/` (SFX02, 04–24 as listed, plus `SFX_pop.mp3` and `SFX_paper_rip.mp3` as unnumbered extras for the CALLOUT and TORN moves). **SFX03_siren is deliberately not seated** (no sirens, per the brief; see "Not seated").

**Nobody has listened to any of these files.** Every choice was made from the source page's title, tags and description plus measurement (loudness, spectrum, pulse/onset analysis, a spectral check for sustained sweeping tones in the ambiences). Treat every file as a candidate until one listening pass in the edit. Where a file is a weaker fit or built from another sound, it is marked **STAND-IN** and says why.

No commercial-library audio, no YouTube rips, no copyrighted score. All sources: Mixkit (free licences), Freesound (CC0), OpenGameArt (CC0), Pixabay (Content License, one file). Music tags were checked for ethnic or religious instruments (oud, duduk, shofar, cantorial, anthem, march): none of the nine files is tagged with any; that check is by tags and analysis only, not by ear.
