## Candidates looked at and not used

- AWM A02462, troops landing at Anzac Cove, 25 April 1915 (https://www.awm.gov.au/collection/C562): "Copyright expired - public domain", but the only file on the page is 639×476. IMG01 uses the larger SLNSW scan instead.
- File:Captured_Turkish_trench_Lone_Pine_1915.jpg (AWM G01126) is public domain, but a soldier has a clearly bandaged arm. Skipped under "no wounded." IMG09 uses AWM A02022 instead.
- File:Anzac, the landing 1915.jpg is George Lambert's painting (1920–22), not a photograph of the landing.
- File:Winston Churchill LCCN2014705084.tif is filed on Commons under the American novelist Winston Churchill, not the statesman.
- File:Mr. and Mrs. Winston Spencer Churchill.jpg is marked public domain in the United States only, and the page says it might not be public domain outside the United States.
- File:Bataille_navale_des_Dardanelles_1915_carte_ancienne.jpg is the same US-only public-domain warning. Not used for IMG19.
- File:Dardanelles_defences_1915.png is the English-label version of IMG19 and is public domain, but it is only 600×620. The SVG was rendered instead.
- Nusret museum-replica photographs on Commons are mostly CC BY-SA. One 9 August 2024 photograph of the replica in the Dardanelles is CC0 (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ottoman_Minelayer_Nusret_in_front_of_TCG_Uluçalireis,_Dardanelles_Strait,_Türkiye.jpg). The historic 1912 photograph was used for IMG05 instead.
- File:Ataturks_Words_wza.jpg is explicitly captioned "Ari Burnu Memorial" but is CC BY-SA 3.0, so it was not used. IMG17 is a CC BY photograph of the same kind of monument.
- File:ANZAC_Cove_2015_02.jpg is CC BY-SA 4.0. File:Lone_Pine_Cemetery_2013.07.26.jpg is CC BY-SA 3.0. Not used.
- File:Mustafa_Kemal_Atatürk_1921.jpg is CC BY-SA 4.0. Not used.
- File:Ataturk_in_1923.jpg is marked public domain but is only 741×957. IMG16 uses the larger March 1923 Rol photograph.
- The Frank and Frances Carpenter Collection photograph of Mustafa Kemal and Latife (LOC cph.3c39361) says only "no known copyright restrictions," not public domain, CC0, or CC BY.
- AWM H17538 (AE2 at sea) is "Copyright expired - public domain" but the page image is about 640 px and the Commons copy is 543×395. IMG07 uses the larger H11559 scan.
- IWM website copies generally sit under the IWM Non-Commercial Licence, which is not public domain, CC0, or CC BY. No IWM file was saved.
