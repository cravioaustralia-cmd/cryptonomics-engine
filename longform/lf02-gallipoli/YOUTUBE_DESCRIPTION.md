In 1915, Australia fought its first great battle as a nation, and lost. Gallipoli became the most famous story Australia has.

But what if the Anzacs had broken through? On one map, we follow the real plan: the back door to Germany, the naval attack of 18 March, the landing at Anzac Cove, the submarine AE2, the August breakout and the escape. Then we play out the what-if with what historians actually think: the Ottoman Empire, Bulgaria, Russia and the Middle East. And the twist: why winning might have cost Australia the story that still brings former enemies together on the same beach.

Which “what if” should we map next? Tell us in the comments.
