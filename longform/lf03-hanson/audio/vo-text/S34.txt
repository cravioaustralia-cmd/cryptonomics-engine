But is One Nation’s rise really that simple? The same polls also show the limits of Pauline Hanson’s support. Roy Morgan estimates that Labor would still beat One Nation fifty-four to forty-six after preferences. Newspoll has fifty-one per cent of voters disapproving of Pauline Hanson herself. And back in nineteen ninety-eight, One Nation also reached twenty-three per cent of the vote in Queensland, then fell apart within a few years.
