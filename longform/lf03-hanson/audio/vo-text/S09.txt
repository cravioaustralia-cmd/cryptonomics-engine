Pauline Hanson’s political breakthrough came in nineteen ninety-six. That year, the Liberal Party chose her to run in the federal seat of Oxley, which one political record describes as a traditionally safe Labor electorate. Before election day, Pauline Hanson wrote to a local newspaper attacking government assistance for Aboriginal Australians, and the Liberal Party disendorsed her as its candidate. But the ballot papers had already been printed, so Pauline Hanson’s name still appeared beside the word Liberal. She won the seat of Oxley, with a swing of more than nineteen per cent.
