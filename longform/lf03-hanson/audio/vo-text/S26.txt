By February twenty twenty-six, an ABC report on One Nation was headlined: “More people say they’ll vote One Nation.” The poll numbers in that ABC report showed the Coalition on eighteen per cent, and One Nation on twenty-seven per cent. In the same month, Pauline Hanson said in an interview that there are no good Muslims, then partly walked the comment back. The Senate censured Pauline Hanson again, and this time, the Coalition voted against the censure.
