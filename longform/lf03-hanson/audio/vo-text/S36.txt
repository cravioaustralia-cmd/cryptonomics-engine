So here’s the pattern in Pauline Hanson’s thirty years in politics. Put her nineteen ninety-six speech next to her twenty sixteen speech, and Pauline Hanson’s message is almost exactly the same. Put the One Nation polls from nineteen ninety-eight next to the polls from twenty twenty-six, and the country around Pauline Hanson looks very different. So, did Pauline Hanson change Australia, or did Australia change around Pauline Hanson? Her supporters and her critics will answer that question very differently.
