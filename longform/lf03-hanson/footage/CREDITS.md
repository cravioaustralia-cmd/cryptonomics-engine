# lf03 footage credits

Real free-licensed video collected for the fix pass. All clips must be **muted** in the edit. Scale to 1920×1080. Prefer these over AI B-roll and over static photos where the narration matches. Do **not** place a real clip on an imagined/what-if beat. Real people only as themselves.

## ParlView / APH broadcast

**Not used.** APH Conditions of Access (https://www.aph.gov.au/News_and_Events/Watch_Read_Listen/Conditions_of_Access) restrict official broadcast material to fair and accurate reports of proceedings, require balanced presentation of differing views, ban digital manipulation, and ban use for political party advertising/election campaigns or commercial sponsorship/advertising. Related terms (https://www.aph.gov.au/Help/Register/Terms_and_conditions) also ban satire or ridicule. AUSParliamentLive YouTube conditions require express written permission to download/reproduce. For this YouTube documentary those terms are too restrictive / unclear, so no ParlView was downloaded.

PEO “About Parliament” chamber explainers on Wikimedia Commons (CC BY 3.0) were inspected but **not** included: low resolution (854×480), Australian Senate/Coat of Arms watermark, and identifiable MPs who are not subjects of the narration.
