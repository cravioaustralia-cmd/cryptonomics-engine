## Named people skipped

- **John Howard.** No Commons file that is both an official portrait from around 2003 and a clearly free licence. `File:Howard John BANNER.jpg` is a Commonwealth portrait the page dates "circa 2001", with licence template `{{cc-by-sa-3.0}}`, but the author line is "© Commonwealth of Australia 2011" and the permission URL (https://www.civicsandcitizenship.edu.au/cce/copyright_notice,9310.html) returned 404, so the licence is unclear. The February 2003 files (`File:John Howard, Feb. 2003-1.jpg` and the related Pentagon pictures) are US Department of Defense press-conference photographs marked public domain. They are not official portraits, so they were not used in place of one.
- **David Oldfield.** The English Wikipedia page "David Oldfield (politician)" has no photograph. The only Commons file titled `File:Oldfield, David.jpg` is a 2010 picture of a Leicester City footballer (CC BY-SA 3.0), not the One Nation co-founder. No freely licensed portrait of the politician was found.
- **David Farley.** `File:David Farley.png` is CC BY 4.0 (The Australia Institute) but the file page says Lee Hanson is in the frame behind him. Not saved: it is a photograph that includes Pauline Hanson's family. `File:David Farley 6 News.jpg` is a YouTube thumbnail of a SIX News video (`{{YouTube CC-BY 4.0}}`). Not saved: news-network material.
