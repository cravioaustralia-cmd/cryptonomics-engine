**Title:** What If Japan Had Invaded Australia in 1942? | IF AUSTRALIA… Ep 1

---

In February 1942, Japanese admirals put a proposal on the table: invade Australia. The idea was real, and Japan's top commanders argued about it for weeks. Then it was rejected.

But what if it hadn't been? On one map, we play out three versions of 1942 with real plans, real numbers and real geography: a small invasion of the north, a full invasion of the east coast, and the plan Japan actually chose, which may have been the most dangerous of all. Then the twist: how the invasion that never came still changed Australia, from its alliances to its cities and the mix of people who live there.

Which "what if" should we play out next? Tell us in the comments.
