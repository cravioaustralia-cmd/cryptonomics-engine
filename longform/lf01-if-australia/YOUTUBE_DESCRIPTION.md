**Title:** What If Japan Had Invaded Australia in 1942? | IF AUSTRALIA… Ep 1

---

In February 1942, Japanese admirals put a proposal on the table: invade Australia. The idea was real, and it was rejected. So what if it hadn't been?

We play out three versions of 1942 on the map: a grab for the north, a full invasion of the east coast, and the plan Japan actually chose, to cut Australia off from America. Then the twist: how an invasion that never came still shaped the Australia we know today.
