Other flags
- PH26 is Aranha presiding at the 2nd session (Commons date "Setembro de 1947"), not the 29 Nov vote. Caption "UN General Assembly, 1947" is accurate; do not caption it "the vote".
- AWM A02684 (often captioned "the charge at Beersheba") is, per AWM, probably a re-enactment filmed/photographed later — not used.
- FT02b is January 1918 (manoeuvres near Esdud), caption "Palestine, 1918" — do not label it as the charge.
