14,000 KILOMETRES — Why Gaza Matters in Australia · Chapter 1: The Vote Australia Cast First

This is an independent documentary made for educational and informational purposes. It does not take a side in the Israeli–Palestinian conflict, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or paid for by any government, political party, advocacy group or community organisation. If you believe anything is inaccurate or unfair to any side, contact [your email] and we will review it promptly.

In November 1947 the United Nations voted on a plan to divide Palestine. The first country to vote yes was Australia. Thirty years earlier, Australian Light Horsemen had charged at Beersheba. This chapter follows how one event became two memories, and how both memories came to Australia.
