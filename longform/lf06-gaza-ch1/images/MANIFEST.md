What each photo shows (checked visually)
- PH01: B&W head-and-shoulders portrait, round glasses, suit and tie; 1940 (Evatt was 46). No text.
- PH26: Oswaldo Aranha standing at the GA rostrum reading, two officials seated either side, microphones, wall map behind; Flushing Meadow hall, Sept 1947 session. Not the 29 Nov vote. No slogans/flags.
- PH02: 4th Light Horse Regiment column with horses moving across open desert plain toward hills, c. 31 Oct 1917. Burned-in strip at bottom "AUSTRALIAN WAR MEMORIAL … A02788" → crop bottom ~5% in edit.
- PH02b: Distant line of Light Horse advancing across flat desert, Oct 1917. Same AWM strip "J06574" at bottom → crop.
- PH02c: Light Horsemen with horses in a hollow below a stone wall, morning after the charge (1 Nov 1917); no casualties visible. AWM strip "P02041.010" at bottom → crop.
- PH03: Dense crowd with raised arms cheering opposite the Tel Aviv museum, 14 May 1948; palm trees, banners (no readable slogan), a small Israeli flag on a balcony, car at right. Non-violent.
- PH04: Women carrying large bundles on their heads walking a dirt road with children; a man and donkey behind; 30 Oct 1948. Dignified, no injured/dead, no weapons visible.
- PH05_small: Smiling, waving Dutch migrants (women, men, children) at a ship's rail on arrival, 1954. No slogans/flags.
- Balance: PH03 and PH04 are from the same archive (Israel GPO), same licence, both B&W 1948, similar resolution → crop both to 16:9 at equal size. Caveat for transparency: both photographers worked for the Israeli GPO; PH04's original caption is "Arab People fleeing from their galilee villages as israeli troops approach".
