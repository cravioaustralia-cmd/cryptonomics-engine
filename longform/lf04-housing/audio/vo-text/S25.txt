Residents who object to new developments have their own defence: many argue that schools, roads and hospitals haven’t kept up with the people already living there. And the building industry’s defence is that builders can only build what gets approved, financed and staffed. According to the O E C D, Australia went into the COVID pandemic with only about four hundred homes for every one thousand people, one of the lowest numbers in the developed world. So who built too little? The evidence points at almost everyone involved.
