In twenty twenty-two, federal, state and local governments, along with builders and unions, agreed to something called the National Housing Accord. The Housing Accord now sets a target of one point two million new homes, in the five years from July twenty twenty-four to June twenty twenty-nine. To stay on track, Australia needed about four hundred and twenty thousand homes in the first twenty-one months. Australia completed three hundred and seven thousand, six hundred and thirty-five.
