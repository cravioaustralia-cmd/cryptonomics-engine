Normally, if your deposit is smaller than twenty per cent of the price, the bank makes you pay for something called lenders mortgage insurance. Lenders mortgage insurance protects the bank, not you, and it can cost thousands of dollars. Under the five per cent Deposit Scheme, the government guarantees part of the loan instead, so first home buyers can buy with just a five per cent deposit and skip that insurance. On the first of October, twenty twenty-five, the government expanded the scheme to first home buyers of all incomes.
