And then the government acted. In the federal budget on the twelfth of May, twenty twenty-six, Treasurer Jim Chalmers announced that from the first of July, twenty twenty-seven, negative gearing will be limited to newly built homes, and the fifty per cent capital gains tax discount will be replaced with a system based on inflation. Properties investors already owned on budget night are protected. On the twenty-fifth of June, twenty twenty-six, the changes passed Parliament with the support of the Greens.
