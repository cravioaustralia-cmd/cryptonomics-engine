The government’s defence is that without the scheme, many first home buyers would struggle to get into the market at all. A spokesperson for Housing Minister Clare O’Neil said the government made no apologies for helping hundreds of thousands of first home buyers while it fixes a supply problem generations in the making. But there’s a risk. A five per cent deposit means a ninety-five per cent loan. So if prices fall, or interest rates rise, recent buyers can end up owing more than their home is worth.
