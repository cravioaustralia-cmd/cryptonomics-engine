Here’s the timeline of the R B A’s decisions. In twenty twenty-five, the R B A cut the cash rate three times, down to three point six per cent. Then inflation came back. In February twenty twenty-six, the R B A started raising the cash rate again. Then again in March. Again in May. And again in September, to four point six per cent. The Australian Council of Trade Unions estimates the September rise alone adds about one hundred and ten dollars a month to the repayments on an average mortgage.
