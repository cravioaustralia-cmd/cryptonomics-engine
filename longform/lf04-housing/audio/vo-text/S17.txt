Put negative gearing and the capital gains tax discount together, and you get a strategy: lose a bit of money every year, pay less tax while you do it, then sell later and pay tax on only half the profit. Critics say the two tax rules turned houses into investment products, and put investors up against first home buyers like Sam at the same auctions. In March twenty twenty-six, a Senate committee reported evidence that the two rules had shifted home ownership away from people who live in their homes, and towards investors.
